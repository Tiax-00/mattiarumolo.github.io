About

I work at the intersection of condensed matter physics and embedded engineering — from fabricating 2D heterostructure transistors in ISO 6 cleanrooms to writing low-power firmware for smart wearable devices.

My M.Sc. curriculum at Politecnico di Milano was co-developed with STMicroelectronics, with a focus on extreme miniaturisation, solid-state device modelling, and advanced MEMS integration. I enjoy problems that live at the boundary between the physical world and the digital one.
